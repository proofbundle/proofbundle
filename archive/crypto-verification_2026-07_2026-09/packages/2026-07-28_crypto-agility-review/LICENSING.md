# Licensing boundary

No project-wide license has been assigned in this review packet. Do not infer a
right to redistribute from technical inclusion alone.

- The package lock records the declared MIT licenses of `@noble/ciphers` and
  `@noble/hashes`; their own notices and source repositories remain
  authoritative.
- Files under `reference/from-scratch/` and `upstream/` came from the supplied
  attachments. This packet preserves their provenance but does not determine
  ownership or grant a license for them.
- The newly authored ProofBundle modules, tests, examples, scripts, documents,
  and Coq files need an explicit license choice from the rights holder before
  external distribution.

Before release, perform a source-by-source rights review, add the selected
license and notices, and confirm that the attached reference/upstream material
may be redistributed. This is a release blocker, not a cryptographic issue.
