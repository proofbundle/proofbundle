date_utc: 2026-08-05T04:18:27Z

RESULT: NOT RUN for cross-implementation interoperability.

Interoperability in the sense the specification means it — the same bundle
verified by a second, independently written implementation — requires a
second implementation. None is available in this environment, so every
interop_status cell in CRYPTOGRAPHIC_SURFACE.csv reads NOT_RUN.

WHAT WAS CHECKED INSTEAD, and what it is worth:

  a) External authoritative vectors. HMAC-SHA-256 against RFC 4231 TC1 and
     HKDF-SHA-256 against RFC 5869 A.1. These ARE cross-implementation
     checks: the expected bytes come from the RFC, not from this code.
     BLAKE2b-512 and BLAKE2s-256 are checked against published digests.
  b) node:crypto as a second implementation for SHA3/SHAKE. crypto/keccak.mjs
     (PURE_MJS) was previously cross-checked against node's OpenSSL-backed
     SHA3 — two independent implementations agreeing.
  c) CLI-to-library round trip. Keys and signatures produced through the CLI
     verify through the library and vice versa (test/integration/cli.test.mjs).
     This is a self-consistency check, NOT interoperability.

NOT CHECKED: whether any other ProofBundle implementation, or any third-party
tool, accepts artifacts produced here. That claim is not made.
