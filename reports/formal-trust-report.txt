5. WHAT A VERIFIED SIGNATURE MEANS HERE:
   That the holder of a private key produced a signature over exactly those
   bytes, subject to ASSUMPTION-SIGNATURE-CORRECTNESS. It does NOT mean the
   signed statement is true, and it does NOT identify who controls the key.
   The CLI prints this scope note on every successful verification.
