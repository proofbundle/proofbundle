Verification surfaces

Evidence is reported by instrument and scope.

  • Generated cases exercise current implementations and outcome classes.
  • Frozen vectors preserve independently addressable conformance evidence.
  • Negative and hostile cases establish deterministic rejection behavior.
  • Formal builds and axiom audits state exactly which proof obligations close and which assumptions remain.
  • Signed certifications bind the applicable evidence to one claim, profile, artifact state, identity, and time.
